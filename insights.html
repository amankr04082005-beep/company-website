01Capabilities

STCS India Strengths and Capabilities

Telecommunications expertise, across the network.

STCS excels in various service domains, showcasing its expertise in the telecommunication industry. Its capabilities include transport equipment across optics and packet technologies, advanced IP/MPLS networking, and comprehensive operations and maintenance services.

The company also provides managed services, remote technical support for swift troubleshooting, technical documentation, staffing solutions and Network Operations Center services to monitor and manage network operations.

STCS’s diverse service expertise positions it as a partner for comprehensive telecommunication solutions.

02Quality & responsibility

Important Facts about STCS

Setting standards in quality, safety and responsibility.

STCS describes its commitment to quality, safety and environmental stewardship through its industry practices and certifications. The company identifies ISO 9001 for quality management, ISO 14001 for environmental management, ISO 45001 for occupational health and safety, and OHSAS 18001:2007.

STCS also describes an in-house specialized laboratory for field training, giving employees practical experience that supports skills, safety and industry learning.

Its 360-degree EPIC (Engineering, Procurement, Installation and Commissioning) lifecycle approach is intended to oversee project phases and support integrated, efficient delivery. Environmental, health and safety practices are described as part of its operating commitment.

03Service coverage

Services Coverage and Strength

A trusted telecommunications partner, close to delivery.

STCS reports a network of more than 500 field engineers and 12 regional branch offices across India, supporting on-site service in a range of locations. The company also describes extending its footprint into an APAC country.

STCS highlights specialized knowledge in wire and wireline technology and a team of more than 300 technical staff deployed at client locations. Its stated 24/7 support model is designed to respond to technical requirements and help minimize downtime.

With a broad service portfolio and a focus on quality, efficiency and responsiveness, STCS aims to support regional projects and cross-border telecommunications initiatives.
